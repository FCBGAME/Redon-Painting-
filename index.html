Who we are

About us

Our mission at Redon Painting is simple: to deliver high-quality work that our clients are proud of.

Every project is different, so we take the time to understand exactly what you need and shape our work around it. From a single room to a full exterior repaint or a commercial space, each job gets the same care and attention.

We keep communication open and honest from your first call to the final coat, so you always know where things stand.

Meet the owner

With over 10 years of experience in the painting trade, our owner brought his craft from New Zealand to Melbourne, where Redon Painting is now proudly based. A decade of hands-on work has shaped our standards: careful preparation, clean finishes and a tidy site from start to finish.
