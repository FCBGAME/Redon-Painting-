Who we are

About us

Our mission at Redon Painting is simple: to deliver high-quality work that our clients are proud of.

Every project is different, so we take the time to understand exactly what you need and shape our work around it. From a single room to a full exterior repaint or a commercial space, each job gets the same care and attention.

We keep communication open and honest from your first call to the final coat, so you always know where things stand.

Meet the owner

With over 10 years of experience in the painting trade, Albert brought his craft from Auckland to Melbourne, where Redon Painting is now proudly based. A decade of hands-on work has shaped our standards: careful preparation, clean finishes and a tidy site from start to finish.

Good to know

Common questions

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the short quote form on this page or call us on 0413 818 803. Tell us about the job, and we will get back to you to talk it through and provide a quote. We may ask for a few photos or a quick video call, or come out and have a look.

Do you work across all of Melbourne?

Yes. We are happy to work all around Melbourne, from the inner city to the outer suburbs. Just tell us your suburb when you get in touch.

Do you paint both homes and businesses?

Yes. We do residential painting for homes, units and townhouses, and commercial painting for offices, shops and other business spaces.

What services do you offer?

Interior and exterior painting, plastering, drywall, fence painting, and deck painting and staining.

Do you need photos of the job?

Not to start with. Send your request and we will call you back. For smaller jobs we may ask for a few photos or a quick video call, and for bigger jobs we are happy to come out and have a look.

How long will my job take?

It depends on the size of the job and the preparation needed. When we talk through the work with you, we will give you an idea of timing.
