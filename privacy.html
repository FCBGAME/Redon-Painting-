Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Redon Painting (ABN 92 757 617 981) respects your privacy. This page explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how we look after it.

What we collect

When you fill in the quote form, we collect the details you choose to give us: your name, phone number, email address, suburb, the type of work you need, any message you write, and any photos you attach. If you call, text or email us, we also keep the details you share with us.

Why we collect it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, discuss the job, prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, carry out the work. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to your enquiry.

How the quote form works

Quote requests are sent through a third-party form service (Web3Forms) to our business email inbox at info@redonpainting.com.au. The website itself does not store your details in a database.

Who we share it with

We only share your information with service providers who help us run the website and our email, and where required by law. We do not share it with anyone else.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads. If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will update this policy.

Keeping your information safe

The website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and your enquiry is delivered to a password-protected business mailbox. We keep your details only as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry or job, or as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. If you have a complaint about how we handled your information, contact us first and we will do our best to put things right. You may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
