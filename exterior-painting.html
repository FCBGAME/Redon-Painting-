How we work

Prepare for the weather

The outside of a building takes a beating, so surfaces are prepared carefully before painting to give the new coat the best chance to last.

The right method for the job

Depending on the surface, we can brush, roll or spray. We own a high-quality spray gun, which helps give an even finish over larger areas.
