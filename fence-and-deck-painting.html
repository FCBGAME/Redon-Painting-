How we work

Prepare the timber

Outdoor timber needs proper preparation so the paint or stain can do its job. We do not skip this step.

Paint or stain, your choice

Some timber suits paint, some suits a stain that lets the grain show through. We will talk through the options with you.

Quick, even coverage

For long fences we can spray, using our own high-quality spray gun, to give an even finish.
